What is a NAD Face Cream?

A nad face cream is a topical moisturiser formulated with NAD+ or NAD+-supporting ingredients. NAD+ is a coenzyme found naturally in the body and is involved in cellular energy processes. In skincare, the idea is to support the appearance of healthy, well-cared-for skin while delivering the everyday benefits you expect from a face cream: hydration, comfort, and a smoother feel.

What is NAD+ Thought to Do in Skin?

NAD+ plays a role in biological pathways linked to energy metabolism and repair processes. Interest in a nad face cream comes from early research into how NAD+ levels relate to skin ageing and overall skin function. However, evidence for topical NAD+ skincare is still emerging, and results can vary. A nad face cream should be viewed as a supportive cosmetic product, not a treatment or guaranteed anti-ageing solution.

Who is a NAD Face Cream For?

A nad face cream can suit adults looking for a daily moisturiser with a science-led approach. It may be a good fit for skin that feels dry, tired, or in need of a more refined skincare routine. As with any face cream, those with sensitive skin should patch test first and introduce it gradually.

How to Use a NAD Face Cream

Apply a nad face cream to clean, dry skin once or twice daily, depending on your routine and skin comfort. Use a small amount over the face and neck, avoiding the eye area unless the product is labelled for that use. In the morning, follow with broad-spectrum sunscreen to help protect your skin’s appearance.
